A starting scenario, not measured surplus or customer demand. Output follows the quotation’s annual generation. Self-consumption / time-of-use sizing also accounts for the entered operating reserve.

Price is a separate all-inclusive upgrade. Changing the bank, model, converter or electrical specifications clears its old price and backup confirmation for review. Changing models also clears warranty terms.

Specifications, efficiencies & manual overrides

Automatic mode uses catalogue rated / usable energy and current. Default 96% DC-to-AC conversion and 15% power derating are editable design assumptions, NOT manufacturer efficiency claims. Select Manual to override derived capacity and AC output.

Operating assumptions & savings

Backup is not automatically a bill saving. Complete every assumption below for an optional first-year storage-only illustration.

This action uses 88% AC round-trip efficiency, 20% reserve, one cycle/day, 365 days, two 4-hour windows and 10-year service life as editable modelling assumptions-not OEM specifications. Enter the source/export tariff and O&M; no savings is inferred from missing values.

Energy is capped by storage capacity, reserve, power, charging source and selected demand. Daily charge plus discharge windows must not exceed 24 hours. Degradation and replacement costs are not modelled; this is not a lifetime ROI forecast.

Subsidy auto-calculates for residential projects per PM Surya Ghar slabs (₹30,000/kW × 2 + ₹18,000 × 1, capped at ₹78,000). RWA/common-facility projects use ₹18,000 per eligible kW (provisional until eligibility is verified). Commercial/industrial projects show nil unless overridden below.
For common facilities. Confirm the 500 kW / 3 kW-per-house limits, including individual residents' rooftop systems, with the approving authority. Enter the eligible common-facility capacity remaining after those limits. Blank uses installed DC capacity up to 500 kW provisionally; 0 means no eligible capacity. An explicit subsidy amount below overrides this calculation.
State top-up is published as a range - ₹25,000–₹60,000 by capacity, max at 3 kW; SMART households (<100 units/month) add ₹17,500 (BPL) / ₹15,000 (SC/ST) / ₹10,000 (others) on the 1 kW benchmark. Verify on MSEDCL I-SMART and enter the figure that applies - the sheet never invents a slab.
