# Advanced optional-system workflow

22 September 2026 — rebuilt BESS and additional-system experience.
Preview branch only. No merge or production deployment authorised.

This document supersedes the initial BESS layout/methodology notes from the same
date. The strict storage economics engine is retained; the customer reports,
automatic configuration workflow and standalone exports are rebuilt.

## Editor and customer experience

- **BESS Yes / No** remains a separate accessible section. Default No keeps the
  solar-only workflow. Turning No on/off preserves draft inputs; New/Reset clears
  them. Missing fields on legacy proposals cannot inherit a previously open quote.
- Select **customer requested**, **recommended upgrade** or **optional alternative**.
  Legacy data without a reason is not silently labelled customer-requested or
  seller-recommended. The new form initially offers Recommended.
- Five native dropdown battery choices and **Custom last**, with manual identity
  entry below. Models and source editions are documented in
  [storage-catalogue.md](storage-catalogue.md). No Excel dependency is introduced.
- Catalogue mode populates specifications, rounds module quantity and limits
  output by the proposed converter. Manual specification mode, a whole-module
  quantity override and independent converter kW control remain available.
- Essential-load sizing needs **load and duration**, not a solar kWp guess.
  Alternatively, an explicitly labelled solar-shifting scenario uses average
  generation and an editable share. Backup target hours start blank.
- Two photo-led BESS pages cover the proposal, connected-energy concept, battery
  bank, usable AC energy, design output, compatibility-gated backup, technical
  basis, separate investment and optional economics. New imagery is captioned
  **AI concept illustration**, not a manufacturer product photograph.
- **Additional System** is a separate Yes/No section: Zero Export, Energy
  Monitoring, EV Charging, DG–Solar Coordination and Custom. One additional system
  per proposal, with editable name, purpose, equipment, scope, exclusions, notes,
  installed price, warranty and delivery. No fabricated system-specific savings.
- Two additional-system pages explain purpose, equipment, operating concept,
  scope, engineering limits and separate commercial basis. Changing a template
  refreshes descriptive content but retains price/commercial terms for review,
  as disclosed beside the control.
- Both features have independent **Include in main proposal** checkboxes. An
  enabled but excluded feature is still available as a standalone download.

The registry, navigation, numbering, Customer View, browser print and PDF agree on
main inclusion. Standalone exports use independent snapshots and Page N of M
numbering; they do not change the live main proposal. Builder and Customer View
both expose the formats. PDF guards reject overflowing supplement content instead
of silently clipping it. Arbitrarily long content cannot fit a fixed A4 page.

Save, reload, duplicate/version, New, Reset, JSON export/import and Customer View
use the canonical form state. Catalogue calculations derive again from those
inputs. Customer View remains subject to the existing browser-local sharing
limitations; this work does not add a hosted multi-device proposal backend.

## Automatic sizing

With efficiencies expressed as fractions:

```
average daily solar energy = annual solar generation / 365
solar-linked energy target = average daily solar energy × selected share
backup energy target = essential load × entered target hours
usable AC energy/module = catalogue usable DC energy × conversion factor
design AC power/module = nominal V × documented current / 1000
                        × (1 − power derating) × conversion factor
recommended modules = ceil(target / usable AC energy/module)
backup-mode modules = max(energy-based modules, ceil(load / design power/module))
proposed output = min(modules × design AC power/module, converter kW limit)
```

A missing converter override uses the solar quotation's inverter kW **only as a
candidate output ceiling**. This does not assert it is battery-compatible.
Invalid nonempty overrides are rejected, not silently replaced with defaults.
Quantities must be whole and within the documented model ceiling. Undersizing,
invalid assumptions and converter overload are flagged. A larger engineered
configuration needs project-specific input, not automatic extrapolation.

Example: a 7 kWp / 10,220 kWh-year quote with US5000 and a 30% scenario targets
8.4 kWh/day. At the initial 96% conversion and 15% power derating assumptions,
two modules provide 9.6 kWh rated, 9.12 kWh usable DC, 8.7552 kWh usable AC and a
7 kW converter-limited design output. At a confirmed compatible 2 kW constant
backup load the full-charge runtime estimate is 4.3776 hours. This is not a
runtime guarantee or a sizing rule based on solar capacity alone.

Selecting another model (including Custom) clears the old storage price,
warranty and engineering confirmation. Changing the derived bank/output clears
stale price and backup confirmation, including when the configuration becomes
invalid. Editing the interface/inverter also resets confirmation. A preparer must
review and reprice the new scope. Manually priced systems are not automatically
repriced from a catalogue or solar ₹/kWp.

## Backup and economic boundaries

```
usable DC energy = rated capacity × usable depth of discharge
usable AC energy = usable DC energy × discharge efficiency
full-charge runtime = usable AC energy / selected constant load
reserve runtime = usable AC energy × reserve fraction / selected load
```

Runtime is withheld until load is positive and within output, AC/DC coupling and
inverter identity are entered, and the preparer explicitly confirms the backup
inverter/isolation/circuit design. Catalogue selection is not certification.
Review motor starting, isolation, protection, location, temperature and applicable
approvals. Ageing, standby loads, changing demand and surge behaviour are not
simulated. The diagram is conceptual, not an installation drawing.

Backup-first mode makes no savings claim. Self-consumption / time-of-use mode
requires complete assumptions, including explicit zeros where applicable:

```
daily delivered AC energy = minimum of:
  usable AC energy × (1 − reserve) × cycles/day
  available charging energy/day × round-trip efficiency
  annual solar generation / operating days × round-trip efficiency [solar source]
  battery-served demand/day
  continuous AC output × discharge hours/day
  charging input power × charging hours/day × round-trip efficiency

annual delivered energy = daily delivered energy × operating days
annual avoided import value = annual delivered energy × avoided import tariff
annual charging cost = annual delivered energy / RTE × source tariff
net year-one storage benefit = avoided import value − charging cost − annual O&M
simple storage payback = installed storage price / positive net year-one benefit
```

The explicit **Use quotation-linked assumptions** button initializes 88% RTE,
20% reserve, 1 cycle/day, 365 days, two 4-hour windows and 10-year assumed service
life. These are editable scenarios, not OEM specifications. Charging power starts
at current storage output; source/demand and avoided tariff can follow the quote.
Foregone export credit/off-peak tariff, O&M and installed price remain manual.
Missing tariffs or maintenance withhold benefit; missing price withholds payback.

The engine rejects nonfinite results, invalid efficiencies, RTE above discharge
efficiency, overloads, charge/discharge windows exceeding 24 hours, invalid
cycles/days/life and negative prices/tariffs/O&M. Nonpositive benefit has no
positive payback; recovery beyond assumed life is labelled accordingly. No
interval dispatch, degradation, replacement, demand-charge, diesel, outage-cost,
discounted cash-flow or combined-system IRR calculation is claimed.

## Price, acceptance and protected solar behaviour

- `finance.js` is unchanged. Storage/additional prices, savings and payment terms
  are **separate** from solar Finance and payment milestones.
- BESS solar-plus-battery investment is explicitly a two-component indicative
  total; it does not include the separately quoted additional system. Prices
  must include the agreed tax, equipment, protection and installation, without
  duplicating already included solar items. No additional subsidy is assumed.
- Solar savings already value generation. Do not add storage benefit to them.
  Zero-export/DG controls may curtail; EV may add demand. Those effects are not
  simulated in the solar model and the report discloses that limitation.
- Included supplements trigger solar-only disclosures in the main report, Power
  Proposal and briefings. Excluded supplements do not change main solar wording.
  Inclusion of a recommended/alternative system alone does not authorise supply;
  the acceptance page requires separately agreed scope.
- Cover and live cover fields, original small-house diagram, exact tracking
  photo, portfolio, solar equipment dropdowns and blank new-proposal identities
  are preserved. Device-dependent speech availability is unchanged.
