# Optional battery storage supplement

Date: 22 September 2026. Preview branch only; no production merge.

## Customer and editor experience

- A separate **Battery storage (BESS)** section is available in Essentials and All settings. The accessible **No / Yes** buttons control one canonical saved boolean.
- **No** is the default, including for legacy proposals. It hides the battery fields and both battery pages. Turning it off retains the draft battery inputs; New and Reset clear them.
- **Yes** adds two A4 pages after Technical Specification: **Battery Storage** and **Storage Assessment**. Base proposals therefore have 15 pages, BESS proposals have 17, and BESS + system comparison + financing have 19. Navigation, page references, footers, Customer View, print and PDF use the same page registry.
- Page one has a clearly captioned, AI-generated **concept illustration**, not an OEM photograph; a separate AC/DC architecture diagram; capacity/energy/power/backup cards; and daylight/evening/outage explanations. The approved small-house solar diagram is unchanged.
- Page two has a usable-energy graphic, three illustrative constant-load backup bars, an investment comparison, charging/discharging concept, bounded storage-only financial illustration and its assumptions.
- The battery and compatible inverter identities are entered for the actual project. Numeric ratings, efficiencies, tariffs, price and operating assumptions start **blank**. Selecting a chemistry or architecture never fills unverified specifications.
- All six existing solar equipment dropdowns, their final Custom options and conditional manual entry remain unchanged.
- Backup-first is the default assessment. Self-consumption and time-of-use assumptions are behind a separate expandable section; they are not necessary to quote a resilience-first supplement.

## Technical method and safeguards

`assets/js/bess.js` contains a separately testable pure `compute(state, solarFinance)` engine and the optional-page / form adapters. It does not modify `Finance.compute`.

With capacity in kWh, power/load in kW and percentage inputs divided by 100:

```
usable DC energy = rated capacity × usable depth of discharge
usable AC energy = usable DC energy × discharge efficiency
full-charge runtime = usable AC energy / selected constant backup load
reserve-only runtime = usable AC energy × reserve fraction / selected load
```

Runtime is withheld until the load is positive, does not exceed continuous AC output, an AC/DC architecture and compatible inverter identity are entered, and the user explicitly marks the inverter/isolation/backup-circuit design confirmed. This is a user-entered engineering status, not a certification by the application. A capacity value alone never proves outage backup. Reserve runtime is a technical estimate and does not require complete tariffs.

The diagram is conceptual, not a wiring plan. Existing grid-tied equipment is not automatically treated as battery-compatible. Final protections, isolation, backed-up circuits, motor starting current, fire safety, installation location and approvals require site/OEM engineering. Runtime assumes full starting charge and constant load; temperature, ageing, auxiliary demand and surge behaviour are not simulated.

## Storage-only operating value

For the self-consumption / time-of-use assessments, every operating and tariff assumption is required, including explicit zero values where appropriate:

```
daily delivered AC energy = minimum of:
  usable AC energy × (1 − reserve) × cycles/day
  entered available charging energy/day × round-trip efficiency
  annual solar generation / operating days × round-trip efficiency [solar source only]
  entered battery-served demand/day
  continuous AC output × discharge hours/day
  charging input power × charging hours/day × round-trip efficiency

annual delivered energy = daily delivered energy × operating days
annual avoided import value = annual delivered energy × avoided import tariff
annual charging opportunity cost = annual delivered energy / RTE × source rate
net year-one benefit = avoided import value − charging cost − annual storage O&M
simple payback = installed storage price / positive net year-one benefit
```

Solar-source rate means foregone export credit, not an assumed zero cost. TOU source rate means eligible off-peak charging tariff. Favourable net-metering may make storage financially unattractive. Zero/negative net benefit produces **No positive payback**. Recovery after the entered service life is labelled **Beyond entered service life**, not a promised investment return. Missing installed price prevents payback and combined investment but does not invent a capital cost.

Validation rejects invalid percentages, nonpositive ratings, overloads, more than 24 total daily charge/discharge hours, RTE greater than discharge efficiency, out-of-range cycles/days/life, negative tariffs/O&M/cost and non-finite arithmetic. Daily figures are representative assumptions bounded by power, energy and annual solar availability; this is **not interval dispatch simulation**. No degradation, replacement cost, discounted cash flow, demand-charge, diesel or outage-cost calculation is included. Simple payback is not lifetime ROI or an IRR.

## Price and scope consistency

- Storage is a **proposal-level, separately priced supplement**, not part of each saved solar option. Applying an existing solar option retains the battery inputs; engineering compatibility must be reviewed for the final configuration.
- The entered storage upgrade price must include applicable tax, conversion equipment, protections and installation, **without duplicating any item already included in the solar quotation**. No additional subsidy is assumed.
- Combined indicative investment is the existing solar net investment plus the known installed storage upgrade. Missing storage price leaves combined investment pending.
- Existing solar savings already value all generation. **Storage benefit is never added to them.** A combined savings model would require consumption intervals and the applicable settlement/tariff model.
- Executive Summary, Savings, Investment, Financing, system comparison and Terms disclose the solar-only scope when BESS is enabled. Solar payment milestones are unchanged. Storage terms must be agreed separately.
- The concise **Power Proposal remains two pages**, with battery identity, capacity, output, separate installed price and a scope notice. It directs the customer to the detailed battery pages; it does not quietly bundle storage into the solar totals.
- English, Hindi and Marathi written/audio briefings add a separate-storage/solar-only pricing explanation. Device speech availability is unchanged; this does not add a hosted speech service.

## Persistence and backward compatibility

The new fields are collected/applied through the existing proposal form and JSON backup pipeline. Reload, duplication, new versions, actual file export/import and Customer View use the same inputs. Loading an old proposal resets missing BESS fields to their defaults, rather than inheriting the previously open proposal. Form collection now excludes non-input helper containers. No persistence schema rewrite is necessary.

Original cover artwork, cover live fields, original system diagram, ten portfolio cards and the exact GitHub-supplied `Tracking.png` remain intact. Finance formulas and new-proposal blank identities are unchanged.
